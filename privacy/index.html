Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android apps published by tekphreak (Michael Hogan), including:

TWITDOWN Threadown

One policy covers all tekphreak apps because they share the same data practices described below. If a specific app's practices ever differ, that app's own listing will link to a page just for it instead of this one.

What these apps do

TWITDOWN and Threadown let you save a copy of a video from a post you already have access to (on X/Twitter or Threads) directly to your own device's storage, after you explicitly share that post's link into the app. Neither app browses, indexes, or accesses any content on your behalf beyond the single link you choose to share.

Data these apps collect

Directly, from you: nothing. These apps have no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, and don't ask you to enter any personal information.

The link you share: used only in-memory, on your device, to fetch the video you asked for. It is not stored by us, sent to our servers, or logged anywhere — we don't operate any server these apps talk to.

The video file itself: downloaded straight from the source platform's own servers to your device's local storage (Movies/ folder). It never passes through, or is copied to, any server we control.

Advertising

Both apps are free and ad-supported using Google AdMob. AdMob (part of Google) may collect and process data such as your advertising ID, general device information, and coarse location, in order to select and measure ads, consistent with Google's own Ads and content policies. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us directly — see Google's Privacy Policy for details on what Google collects and how you can control it (including via your device's ad settings).

Permissions these apps request

Storage access (to save the downloaded video), notification access (to show download progress), and network access (to fetch the video and load ads). None of these are used to collect or transmit personal data beyond what's described above.

Children's privacy

These apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Third-party platforms

TWITDOWN and Threadown are independent, unofficial tools and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by X Corp (Twitter/X) or Meta Platforms, Inc. (Threads). All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date above.
